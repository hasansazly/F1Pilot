# AI safety and legal boundaries

F1Pilot provides information organization and preparation support. It is not a lawyer, government authority, or DSO. It must never decide that a student is eligible, compliant, authorized, admitted, or approved, and must never guarantee an outcome.

## Demonstration rule

`opt-preparation / demo-1.0.0` uses configurable fictional planning offsets: preparation −105 days, illustrative opening −90 days, illustrative outer boundary +60 days, and an additional recommendation +30-day boundary if the student confirms that date. These offsets demonstrate explainable calculation; they are not an independently validated current legal rule. CPT generates a personal −60-day preparation buffer only. Neither process decides authorization.

The official reference is [USCIS OPT guidance](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training-opt-for-f-1-students). Retrieval was attempted on 2026-09-12; the page returned HTTP 403 to the research tool. Accordingly the interface explicitly marks the effective legal date unverified and requires DSO verification. Do not upgrade the review status without a substantive official-source review. The fictional Northstar email is a demo fixture, never an authoritative real university policy.

## Extraction and answers

Extraction is local and deterministic. The 88% value describes a heuristic labeled-field parse score, not calibrated factual certainty; manual entries have no model confidence. The student must inspect the source and confirm separately. Original transcript and correction history are preserved.

The local assistant retrieves confirmed student facts and versioned event explanations; it cites source locations and approved official references. It is intentionally limited, not a general legal chatbot. It states missing inputs, assumptions, and limitations and refuses direct legal-guarantee requests. It cannot browse current policy. Document and email instructions are treated as data, never executable commands. No private document text enters a model or analytics service.

## Approval gates

Draft creation is preparation only. Approval records a local decision and unlocks file download. No email service, account calendar, immigration filing or government submission is connected. A future integration must not treat an earlier generic instruction or upload as approval to send. Capture the exact payload and destination, enforce approval server-side, then separately record execution and failures.

## Escalation

Contradictory dates, unusual evidence, unknown requirements, employment start inconsistencies and current-rule ambiguity should result in questions for a DSO or qualified professional. Travel admission and visa decisions belong to government authorities.
